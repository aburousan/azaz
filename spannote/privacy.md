# Spannote privacy

*Last updated 15 October 2026. [Back to Spannote](/spannote/)*

Spannote is a notebook app. It has no account, no ads and no tracking of its own. Your notes are files on your device, and they leave it only when you choose to send them somewhere.

## What is stored, and where

Notebooks, pictures, PDFs you import, sound recordings and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them, so keep a backup.

## Backups and sync (only if you switch them on)

You can back up to a folder you pick (on the device or a memory card), to your own WebDAV server such as Nextcloud, to your Google Drive, or to a private GitHub repository you own. Files go straight from your device to the place you chose. The app's maker never receives them. Passwords and tokens you enter for these are kept on your device.

## Microphone

The microphone is used only while you are recording, after you press the microphone button and allow it. Recordings are saved inside the notebook on your device.

## Camera and pictures

When you take a photo, Android's camera app takes it and gives it to Spannote; Spannote does not use the camera itself. Pictures you add are copied into the notebook.

## Handwriting reading

To search handwriting and work out maths, Spannote uses Google's ML Kit handwriting recognition, which runs on your device. The first time, it downloads a language model (about 20 MB) from Google. Your writing is read on the device and is not sent to Google or anyone else. Google's ML Kit library may send anonymous usage and performance information to Google; see Google's ML Kit terms for details. You can turn handwriting search off in Settings › Search.

## Internet

The app uses the internet only to download the handwriting model and, if you set them up, for backups and sync.
